# What happened to Artisan

This is the case that prompted this kit. It rests on one report: TechCrunch, 7 January 2026, by Julie Bort. The reasons below are the account of Artisan's CEO, Jaspar Carmichael-Jack, as told to TechCrunch. The report says LinkedIn did not immediately respond to its request for comment, so LinkedIn's own version is not on record there. Full link and quotes are in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).

## What the report says

- Artisan is an AI SDR startup, known for its "Stop hiring humans" billboards. Its AI agent, Ava, does outbound sales.
- LinkedIn's email reached Artisan on Friday evening, December 19 (2025).
- The company page, employee profiles and executive posts showed a "This post cannot be displayed" message.
- The CEO said LinkedIn's "enforcement team" restricted the accounts completely while it reviewed them.
- Viral posts said the cause was AI spam. TechCrunch reported that was not the reason.

## The two reasons, as the CEO told it

1. LinkedIn objected to Artisan using LinkedIn's name on its website. Artisan had used the name to compare some of its data features with LinkedIn's.
2. LinkedIn alleged Artisan was using data brokers who had scraped the site without permission.

## What Artisan did, as the CEO told it

1. It removed all mentions of LinkedIn from its website.
2. It verified that its data partners were operating in compliance with LinkedIn's policies.
3. It worked with LinkedIn for about 2 weeks and was reinstated.

## What the report does not tell you

- It does not give LinkedIn's own reasons.
- It does not say which data brokers were involved, or whether the allegation was proven.
- It does not say how LinkedIn detected any of this.
- One company's outcome is not a rule. Fixing the same things does not promise you the same result.

## A second case, self reported only

HeyReach, a company that sells LinkedIn outreach software, wrote on its own blog that on March 25, 2026 LinkedIn removed its company page and restricted the profiles of 4 executives. This is self reported. HeyReach has a commercial interest in how the story is told, and the post has not been independently confirmed. The same post states volume numbers for LinkedIn. This kit does not repeat them, because LinkedIn does not publish them. One line in that post is worth noting even so: "There is no approved outreach automation tool".
