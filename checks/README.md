# The 30 checks

Each check tells you what to look at, how to check it, the pass or fail test, the fix and the source.

**Priority** is this kit's own judgement about what to do first. **Based on** says whether the check comes from a LinkedIn rule, a law or plain operating practice.

Score your answers with [the audit sheet](../audit/HOW-TO-SCORE.md).
